Inside Sparkco

Show the group different ways to be together online.

The same platform can support different modes of presence: broad participation across the space, focused speaker frames, branded demo rooms, or recurring hubs people return to.

Focused presence without losing the room

Large docking frames can bring a few people into focus while the wider group remains visible around them — useful for mentoring, presenting and guided discussion.

Branded rooms for demos and customer journeys

Use a tailored environment for product walkthroughs, mentor meetings, office hours, or a branded experience that feels alive instead of static.

Small groups inside private spaces

Divide a large group into structured quartets or teams, each with its own private space, then bring everyone back together as the process moves forward.

Hubs, lobbies and recurring homes

Create a sense of continuity with anchor spaces that welcome people back and connect one encounter to the next.
